## Jcode Anthropic interoperability reference

The experimental Anthropic subscription integration is adapted from the OAuth and Anthropic transport implementation in [Jcode](https://github.com/1jehuang/jcode), as present in the [gfx-labs fork](https://github.com/gfx-labs/jcode) at commit `02777ce1bea392f03af4eda48c5292bb48946646`.

Reference modules include `crates/jcode-base/src/auth/oauth.rs`, `crates/jcode-provider-core/src/anthropic.rs`, and the Anthropic provider/runtime crates. This attribution does not imply endorsement by Anthropic or guaranteed support for third-party subscription authentication.
